# 08 — Scene Design Documents

Comprehensive design specs for every panel. Each entry covers lighting, composition, content,
context, framing, camera movement, animations, color palette, design principles, and story beat.

## Chapter 1 — The King's Table

### 1-1: Babylon Cityscape (svg: `babylon_city`)
- **Lighting**: Low-angle sunset from behind the ziggurat, long shadows across terracotta rooftops. Warm amber glow on walls facing the river.
- **Composition**: Wide establishing shot. Euphrates curves through the bottom third. Ziggurat dominates the left third, leaning back. Houses climb the right slope in diminishing scale.
- **Content**: The great city of Babylon at twilight — mud-brick walls, flat rooftops, the famous Ishtar Gate visible in the distance.
- **Context**: Daniel has just been taken captive. This is the foreign city that will become both his exile and his stage.
- **Framing**: 300×200 viewBox, full-frame. Foreground rooftops occupy bottom 20%, city sprawl fills middle 60%, sky + ziggurat top 20%.
- **Camera**: Static, no movement. Depth created through layered detail (rooftops → walls → gates → tower).
- **Animation**: Subtle parallax only. No key animation.
- **Palette**: Warm earth (#5c4033 → #2b1d0c), gold accents on gates (#FFD84D), river reflections (#4ECDC4 at 10% opacity).
- **Principles**: Establish place before character. Let the city feel ancient and overwhelming.
- **Story**: "Babylon, 605 BCE. The young men of Judah had been taken far from home."

### 1-2: Training House Interior (svg: `training_house`)
- **Lighting**: Oil lamps on the left wall cast warm pools of light. Shadows on the right recede into cool blue darkness.
- **Composition**: Medium shot from behind the captives. Table with scroll and stylus in foreground. Shelves of papyrus line the back wall.
- **Content**: Young men (Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, Azariah) seated at a low table learning Babylonian literature.
- **Context**: The king's official training program — an attempt to remake them as Babylonian nobility.
- **Framing**: Centered on the three figures, slightly off-kilter to feel cramped and institutional.
- **Camera**: Static. Slight Dutch tilt (2-3 degrees) to convey unease.
- **Animation**: Parallax on shelves (depth 0.4) vs. figures (depth 0.8).
- **Palette**: Warm lamp glow (#FFD84D), cool stone (#2a1a0a), papyrus (#f5f0e6), robe colors (#8b5a2b, #4a4a4a).
- **Principles**: Contrast warmth of learning with coldness of captivity. Use shadows to show they're between two worlds.
- **Story**: "King Nebuchadnezzar ordered his best captives trained for royal service."

### 1-3: Royal Table (svg: `royal_table`)
- **Lighting**: Overhead chandelier of brass rods and oil lamps. Strong top-down light creates sharp shadows under the table.
- **Composition**: High-angle view of a long table. Golden platters of food and goblets of wine. The king's golden cup at dead center.
- **Content**: The royal dining hall. Feast spread with exotic fruits, spiced meats, and wine flagons.
- **Context**: The king's food — the test of obedience before Daniel proposes the alternative.
- **Framing**: Overhead, slightly rotated to show table length. King's cup is the brightest object.
- **Camera**: Static overhead. Parallax on hanging lamps (depth 0.9), table (depth 0.5), floor (depth 0.1).
- **Animation**: Gentle bobbing of lamp flames. Parallax only otherwise.
- **Palette**: Rich gold (#FFD84D), deep burgundy (#8B0000), burnished bronze (#cd7f32), dark wood (#1e0f0a).
- **Principles**: Show excess and temptation. Make the food look abundant and desirable.
- **Story**: "You will eat from MY royal table — the finest food and wine in all Babylon!"

### 1-5: Daniel Decides (svg: `daniel_decides`)
- **Lighting**: Single shaft of light from a high window illuminates Daniel alone at the table.
- **Composition**: Close-up on Daniel's face and hands. Half in shadow, half in light. The food platter is just out of focus behind him.
- **Content**: Daniel at the royal table, refusing the king's food. His expression is resolute but not angry.
- **Context**: The moment of decision — loyalty to God vs. loyalty to the king's table.
- **Framing**: Tight on Daniel's upper body. The platter is a blurred backdrop. The window light is the key light source.
- **Camera**: Static. Slight push-in on the choice moment.
- **Animation**: Subtle glow pulse on the window light. Parallax on background elements.
- **Palette**: Stark contrast — warm gold light (#FFD84D) on cool blue shadows (#1a0e06). Daniel's robes are neutral brown (#8b5a2b).
- **Principles**: One light source = one choice. Show the moral weight through lighting.
- **Story**: "I cannot eat the king's food. Not because I'm ungrateful. But because there's one thing I won't give up."

## Chapter 2 — The Dream

### 2-1: Throne Room — The Dream (3D: `throne_room`)
- **Lighting**: Royal torches on walls cast warm orange light. Moonlight streams through high arches.
- **Composition**: Wide shot of the throne room. Four towering columns frame the dais. Nebuchadnezzar stands at the dais, agitated.
- **Content**: The king recounting his troubled dream. Courtiers look on with concern.
- **Context**: The crisis point — the king is afraid and demands an impossible answer.
- **Framing**: Camera at medium height, slightly below the throne to emphasize power.
- **Camera**: Cinematic orbit. Slow 180° sweep around the throne area over 8 seconds.
- **Animation**: Torch flames flicker. Camera slowly orbits. Nebuchadnezzar's cape has a subtle wind animation.
- **Palette**: Deep purple shadows (#2a1a0a), warm torch (#FF6B5B), gold (#FFD84D), stone gray (#8b5a2b).
- **Principles**: Power and fear. Make the throne room feel imposing and ancient.
- **Story**: "The king had a dream that terrified him."

### 2-2: Royal Court (svg: `royal_table` or dedicated `babylon_court`)
Actually, the SVG scene key for this line is `babylon_court`.
- **Lighting**: Torch sconces along the walls. The king is lit from below by firelight.
- **Composition**: The king at center, surrounded by his court. Faces turn toward him — fear, confusion.
- **Content**: The king's decree — tell me the dream or die. The death sentence hangs in the air.
- **Context**: The impossible demand. Even the king's own wise men are terrified.
- **Framing**: Tight crowd composition. The king is slightly elevated.
- **Camera**: Static. Parallax on torch sconces (depth 0.9), crowd (depth 0.5), walls (depth 0.2).
- **Animation**: Torch flicker. Subtle crowd shiver animation.
- **Palette**: Fear-inducing — deep red (#8B0000) shadows, harsh gold (#FFD84D) highlights, warm torch (#FF6B5B).
- **Principles**: Convey terror through lighting and composition. The king's face should be half-shadow.
- **Story**: "Interpret my dream — or ALL the wise men of Babylon die."

- **Context**: The moment of turning to God instead of guessing.
- **Framing**: Through the window frame. Daniel is small against the vast city below.
- **Camera**: Static. Parallax on window stars (depth 0.95), Daniel (depth 0.7), city (depth 0.3).
- **Animation**: Gentle prayer glow. Subtle star twinkle.
- **Palette**: Cool blue (#4ECDC4), warm lamp (#FFD84D), city lights (#cd7f32), night (#03010a).
- **Principles**: Intimacy. Show one man talking to God under the vast night.
- **Story**: "No human could answer. But Daniel didn't guess. He prayed first."

- **Composition**: The colossal golden image on the plain of Dura. Thousands of people in rows below.

- **Context**: The setup for the friends' test. The image demands worship.
- **Framing**: Extreme wide shot. The statue fills the center third, people are dots below.
- **Camera**: 3D scene (`golden_statue`) if available, otherwise SVG with parallax.
- **Animation**: Slow camera orbit around the statue. Flags flutter in the wind.
- **Palette**: Blinding gold (#FFD84D), desert earth (#8b5a2b), blue sky (#87CEEB overlay), people in muted colors.
- **Principles**: Show the absurd scale of the king's pride. Make the statue look imposing but false.
