# New playable game ideas

Open [the collection](new-game-ideas.html). All six pages are self-contained and also work directly from disk. They use original code and SVG illustrations; the linked repository supplied mechanic references, not copied code. Every game has Hint, Try again, Extra challenge, Skip and a completion screen.

- [Noah’s Pair Rescue](noah-pair-rescue.html): Find matching animal pairs for the ark. Tap two cards. Remember where each animal is hiding. Reference: https://github.com/he-is-talha/html-css-javascript-games/tree/main/22-Memory-Card-Game.
- [Gideon’s Lantern Pattern](gideon-lantern-pattern.html): Light every lantern. Tapping one lantern also changes its neighbours above, below, left and right. Reference: https://github.com/he-is-talha/html-css-javascript-games/tree/main/50-Lights-Out-Game.
- [Esther’s Secret Seal](esther-secret-seal.html): Discover the three-symbol seal. Change a symbol by tapping it, then check your arrangement. Reference: https://github.com/he-is-talha/html-css-javascript-games/tree/main/38-Mastermind-Game.
- [Creation Picture Grid](creation-picture-grid.html): Reveal a picture of light. Row and column clues tell you how many filled squares belong together. Tap squares, then check. Reference: https://github.com/he-is-talha/html-css-javascript-games/tree/main/49-Nonogram-Game.
- [Nehemiah’s Gate Puzzle](nehemiah-gate-puzzle.html): Slide the numbered stones into order, 1–8. Only a stone beside the empty space can move. Reference: https://github.com/he-is-talha/html-css-javascript-games/tree/main/37-Sliding-Puzzle-Game.
- [Ruth’s Bread Workshop](ruth-bread-workshop.html): Gather grain and water. Craft flour, then bread. Share two loaves with the household. Reference: local Crafting_v1 and Crafting_v2.

These are playable design prototypes. They do not change the live story engines or claim validated suitability for a particular age.
