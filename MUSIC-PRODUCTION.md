# Bible Stories Music Production Pack

Create the tracks in [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) using **Add files → Create music**, or use [Google MusicFX](https://labs.google/fx/).

## Shared sound

Append this direction to every prompt:

> Original instrumental storybook Americana. Warm finger-picked acoustic guitar, gentle fiddle, mandolin, upright bass, brushed percussion, and very restrained pedal steel. Intimate human phrasing with a cinematic biblical atmosphere. No vocals, choir, humming, spoken words, lyrics, or recognisable melody. Approximately 90–120 seconds. Steady ending that can be edited into a seamless loop. Leave headroom and avoid heavy mastering.

Do not name a particular recording artist in the generation prompt. The desired identity should come from the instruments, arrangement, tempo, emotion, and production language above.

## Delivery format

For every cue, provide both versions with the identical basename:

1. `.ogg` — preferred source for seamless browser looping.
2. `.mp3` — compatibility fallback.

Use 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz stereo. Trim leading silence, remove trailing silence, match the loop boundary, and loudness-match the complete set before delivery.

Files: `Ch03_Hammering_Toward_The_Rain.ogg`, `Ch03_Hammering_Toward_The_Rain.mp3`

Files: `Ch04_Two_By_Two.ogg`, `Ch04_Two_By_Two.mp3`

> Curious, warm procession music as animals arrive. Playful mandolin, pizzicato bass, light fiddle, brushed percussion, and gentle acoustic guitar. Wonder rather than comedy; busy but not frantic.

### Chapter 5 — When the Door Closed

Files: `Ch05_When_The_Door_Closed.ogg`, `Ch05_When_The_Door_Closed.mp3`

> Suspense before the storm. Sparse guitar, distant low fiddle, slow bass pulse, and isolated percussion. The final door closes, the outside world becomes quiet, and the first rain begins.

### Chapter 6 — Forty Nights of Thunder

Files: `Ch06_Forty_Nights_Of_Thunder.ogg`, `Ch06_Forty_Nights_Of_Thunder.mp3`

> Powerful storm music driven by low strings, heavy brushed drums, urgent acoustic rhythm, and turbulent fiddle. Convey endurance inside the ark, not an action spectacle. Include calmer passages so dialogue remains readable.

### Chapter 7 — Wings Over the Water

Files: `Ch07_Wings_Over_The_Water.ogg`, `Ch07_Wings_Over_The_Water.mp3`

> Patient waiting after disaster. Gentle guitar, open fiddle harmonics, soft mandolin, and a slow pulse. A raven searches, a dove returns, then finally carries an olive leaf. Hope should arrive quietly.

### Chapter 8 — Mud Beneath the Ramp

Files: `Ch08_Mud_Beneath_The_Ramp.ogg`, `Ch08_Mud_Beneath_The_Ramp.mp3`

> Cautious release into a changed world. Warm acoustic ensemble, light walking rhythm, birdlike fiddle figures, and grounded bass. Relief, mud, sunlight, and animals stepping onto land.

### Chapter 9 — Rainbow Over the Altar

Files: `Ch09_Rainbow_Over_The_Altar.ogg`, `Ch09_Rainbow_Over_The_Altar.mp3`

> Reverent and luminous. Begin with a humble guitar-and-fiddle altar theme, then open into warm mandolin, bass, and broad sustained harmony as the rainbow appears. Covenant, gratitude, and peace without grand orchestration.

### Chapter 10 — Vines After the Flood

Files: `Ch10_Vines_After_The_Flood.ogg`, `Ch10_Vines_After_The_Flood.mp3`

> A mature, bittersweet epilogue. Earthy guitar, reflective fiddle, soft bass, and restrained percussion. New growth and human weakness coexist. End with sober hope and an unfinished sense of generations continuing.

## Integration checklist

- Confirm every `.ogg` and `.mp3` pair has the same musical edit and loop points.
- Put OGG first in each story's `audio` array.
- Keep background music near 30% playback volume beneath dialogue.
- Add every delivered file to its story's precache manifest.
- Test chapter jumps, mute persistence, start-screen autoplay handling, and offline playback.
